In Browser (Normal DOM)

The DOM is the tree-like structure that the browser creates from your HTML.

Example:

<div id="app">
  <h1>Hello</h1>
  <button>Click</button>
</div>


Browser makes an object tree:

Document
↳ div (id="app")
   ↳ h1 ("Hello")
   ↳ button ("Click")

👉 When we say “DOM” in browser context → we are talking about this tree stored in browser memory.
